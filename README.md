## The problem

Who: people who work from paperwork that has to agree with itself, such as an
operations or finance person at a small business checking a purchase order
against a supplier's invoice and delivery note, or anyone comparing two
versions of a contract or quote.

What they do today: they open each document and compare them by eye or in a
spreadsheet. Mistakes (a wrong price, a short delivery, a missing page) are
caught late or not at all.

What better looks like: drop in two or three PDFs and get one answer within a
minute: do they agree? If not, which line and which document disagree, with the
exact text quoted from each so a person can check it in seconds.

## What I don't know yet

- Whether small businesses feel this pain enough to want a tool for it
- Whether their documents are clean PDFs or scans and photos
- Whether they would trust a tool with their documents
